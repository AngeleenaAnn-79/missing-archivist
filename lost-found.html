The Locked Box

At the back of the Lost & Found sits a steel box with four dials. A card is taped to the lid:

"Four rooms, four words. Use them in the order I left them."

Click.

Inside is a brass key and a note in Mira's handwriting:

"You found me. I'm safe. I took the original ledger to the old clock tower, to the top of the spiral stairs, so no one could sell it. Come up and I'll explain everything."

Case closed.
