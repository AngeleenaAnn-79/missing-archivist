The Missing Archivist

Head archivist Mira Voss hasn't been seen since Tuesday. Her desk was left open, and one note was pinned to the catalog board:

"She always filed things under the wrong letter."

Follow her trail room by room. Each page hides a word, and that word is the password to the next room.
